What is Anemal?

Anemal runs the day-to-day operations of a veterinary clinic: booking appointments, keeping medical records, tracking drug and supply inventory, billing clients, and — for clinics with more than one location — coordinating stock transfers and reporting across branches. It also covers veterinary-specific workflows most generic clinic software skips: inpatient/hospitalization care logs, a grooming queue, and a blood-donor/transfusion registry.

Every clinic's data is completely isolated from every other clinic on the platform (multi-tenancy) — no clinic can ever see another clinic's patients, staff, or financial records. Within a clinic, what a staff member can see and do is controlled by their role (built-in or custom), enforced on the server, not just hidden in the menu.

🐾 Features Overview

What Anemal does for your clinic, module by module — in plain language. For step-by-step instructions, see the User Manual and Admin Setup Guide.

Coming later (needs external provider credentials)

These are built and scoped but intentionally switched off until your clinic (or the platform operator) connects a real payment/messaging provider — see Progress → What's Next for status:

  • Automatic SMS / LINE appointment reminders — the reminder is created and queued; actual delivery is off by default until a messaging provider is connected.
  • Card payment processing — cash, bank transfer, and PromptPay QR are fully live today; automated credit-card processing (e.g. Omise) is planned for a later phase.

Who can do what

Every clinic starts with three built-in roles — Clinic Admin (full access), Doctor (clinical work — EMR, prescriptions, inpatient care), and Receptionist/Staff (front-desk operations — appointments, billing, inventory, dispensing). A Clinic Admin can also create custom roles with a hand-picked set of permissions for a specific job (e.g. a groomer who should only see the Grooming queue). See the User Manual's Login & Dashboard section for how role affects what you see, or the Admin Setup Guide for how to assign roles.

Anemal — Clinic Staff User Manual

Touch-first tablet + web browser. Covers day-to-day use of every clinic module.

1. Login & Dashboard Overview

1.1 Logging In (Multi-Tenant)

Each clinic operates under its own isolated tenant. Your clinic URL is unique to your organization — no data is shared between clinics.

  1. Open your browser and navigate to your clinic's URL (e.g., https://dev-clinic.anemal.app).
  2. Enter your Username and Password in the login form.
  3. Click Sign In.

Note: Each staff account belongs to exactly one clinic tenant. If you work across multiple clinics, you will have a separate login credential for each.

Warning: Never share your password. Each login is tied to your personal role and audit trail. All actions are logged under your account.

1.2 Language Toggle (Thai / English)

Anemal supports full Thai and English interfaces. To switch languages:

  1. Look for the language selector in the top navigation bar (upper-right corner).
  2. Click it and choose ภาษาไทย (Thai) or English.
  3. The interface updates immediately — no page reload required.

Tip: Language preference is stored per session. Set it once when you log in each day.

1.3 Dashboard Widgets

After login you land on the Dashboard. The dashboard gives you a real-time snapshot of clinic activity:

WidgetWhat It Shows
Today's AppointmentsCount and list of scheduled visits for the current day
Revenue SummaryToday's collected payments and running monthly total
Inventory AlertsItems at or below reorder threshold, plus expiry warnings
Quick ActionsShortcuts to book appointment, register new patient, create invoice

1.4 Role-Based Menu Visibility

The sidebar menu shows only the sections your role can access:

RoleAccessible Sections
Receptionist (clinic_staff)Dashboard, Pets, Appointments, Billing
DoctorDashboard, Pets, Appointments, EMR, Inventory (view)
Clinic AdminAll sections including Settings, Users, Branches

Note: If a menu item is not visible, your role does not have permission for that section. Contact your Clinic Admin to adjust your access level.

2. Managing Pets & Owners

2.1 Adding a New Owner and Pet

Step 1 — Register the owner:

  1. Click Pets in the left sidebar.
  2. Click the New Owner button (top-right).
  3. Fill in the Owner Name, Phone Number, and Email fields.
  4. Optionally enter the Address.
  5. Click Save Owner.

Step 2 — Add the pet:

  1. On the owner's profile page, click Add Pet.
  2. Enter Pet Name, Species, Breed, Date of Birth, and Sex.
  3. Enter the Microchip / Chip ID if the pet has been chipped.
  4. Click Save Pet.

Tip: You can add multiple pets to a single owner record. There is no limit on the number of pets per owner.

2.2 Pet Photo Upload

  1. Open the pet's profile by clicking the pet's name.
  2. Click the camera icon or the Upload Photo area on the pet card.
  3. On tablet: tap the icon — you can use the device camera or choose from the gallery.
  4. On desktop: a file picker opens. Select a JPEG or PNG image (max 5 MB).
  5. The photo saves automatically and appears on the pet card and in the EMR header.

2.3 Searching for Patients

Use the Search bar at the top of the Pets screen:

  • Search by owner name (first or last name)
  • Search by pet name
  • Search by Chip ID (microchip number)

Results appear as you type. Click any result row to open that pet's profile.

Tip: For fastest results when a client arrives, search by phone number prefix — the system matches partial numbers.

2.4 Editing and Archiving Records

To edit a pet or owner record:

  1. Open the pet or owner profile.
  2. Click the Edit button (pencil icon).
  3. Modify the desired fields.
  4. Click Save.

To archive a pet record (e.g., deceased):

  1. Open the pet profile.
  2. Click More Options (three-dot menu).
  3. Select Archive Pet.
  4. Confirm the action.

Warning: Archiving a pet hides it from active search results but preserves all medical history. This action can be reversed by a Clinic Admin.

3. Scheduling Appointments

3.1 Calendar Views

The Appointments screen offers two calendar layouts:

  • Day View: Shows all time slots for the selected date, grouped by doctor.
  • Week View: Shows a 7-day grid — useful for spotting availability at a glance.

Switch between views using the Day / Week toggle buttons at the top of the calendar.

3.2 Booking an Appointment

  1. Navigate to Appointments in the sidebar.
  2. Click an empty time slot in the calendar, or click the New Appointment button.
  3. In the booking panel:
    • Select the Pet (search by name or owner).
    • Select the Doctor from the dropdown.
    • Confirm the Date and Time.
    • Choose the Appointment Type (e.g., General Checkup, Vaccination, Surgery).
    • Add any Notes for the doctor.
  4. Click Book Appointment.

Tip: Double-booked slots are prevented automatically. If a slot turns grey while you are filling the form, another user has just booked it — choose a different time.

3.3 Editing or Cancelling an Appointment

To edit:

  1. Click the appointment block in the calendar.
  2. Click Edit in the detail panel.
  3. Adjust the time, doctor, or type.
  4. Click Save Changes.

To cancel:

  1. Click the appointment block.
  2. Click Cancel Appointment.
  3. Select a Cancellation Reason from the dropdown.
  4. Confirm.

Warning: Cancelled appointments cannot be restored. If the client reschedules, create a new booking.

3.4 SMS Notification Trigger

When booking or editing an appointment, toggle Send SMS Reminder to on. Once a real messaging provider is connected, the system will send an automated reminder to the owner's registered phone number 24 hours before the visit.

⚠Coming Soon (Phase 11): SMS and LINE OA dispatch are not yet active. The toggle is present in the UI and the reminder is created and queued, but delivery is disabled until a real provider is connected. See the Admin Setup Guide for the same note.

Note: SMS notifications require an active SMS integration. Contact your Clinic Admin if the toggle is greyed out.

4. Electronic Medical Records (EMR)

4.1 Opening a Patient Chart

  1. From the calendar, click an appointment that is checked in.
  2. Click Open Chart or navigate to EMR in the sidebar and search for the patient.
  3. The patient chart opens showing the pet's profile, weight history, and previous visits.

4.2 SOAP Notes

Each visit record follows the standard SOAP structure:

SectionPurposeExample
SubjectiveOwner's reported complaint"Vomiting for 2 days, reduced appetite"
ObjectiveDoctor's physical findingsTemperature 39.5°C, heart rate 110 bpm
AssessmentDiagnosis or differential listGastroenteritis, rule out parvovirus
PlanTreatment, medications, follow-upMetronidazole 3 days, recheck in 7 days
  1. Click on the relevant SOAP field.
  2. Type or dictate your notes.
  3. The record auto-saves every 30 seconds. You can also click Save Draft manually.

4.3 Anatomy Canvas (Tablet — Stylus Drawing)

The anatomy canvas lets you annotate a body diagram directly on a tablet using a stylus or finger.

  1. In the EMR view, scroll to the Anatomy section.
  2. Tap the body diagram area to activate the canvas.
  3. Use your stylus or finger to draw markings (wounds, swellings, lesion sites).
  4. Use the Color and Brush Size controls in the canvas toolbar.
  5. Tap Save Annotation to attach the drawing to the current visit record.

Tip: Each annotation is saved as an image linked to the specific visit. Previous visit annotations are viewable in the history timeline.

4.4 Prescription Entry and Auto-Deduct Inventory

  1. In the EMR, click Add Prescription under the Plan section.
  2. Search for the medication by name or barcode.
  3. Enter the Dosage, Frequency, and Duration.
  4. Click Add to Prescription.
  5. Repeat for additional medications.
  6. Click Finalize Prescription.

When you finalize, the system automatically deducts the prescribed quantities from the clinic's inventory in real time.

Warning: If a medication shows Out of Stock, you cannot add it to the prescription. Coordinate with staff to restock before completing the record, or prescribe an alternative.

Note: Inventory deduction happens at the branch level. If your clinic has multiple branches, stock is tracked separately per branch.

4.5 Viewing the History Timeline

At the bottom of any patient chart, the History Timeline shows all past visits in reverse chronological order. Each entry shows:

  • Visit date and attending doctor
  • Chief complaint summary
  • Diagnoses and prescriptions
  • Attached anatomy annotations (tap to expand)

Click any history entry to expand the full SOAP notes for that visit.

5. Managing Inventory

5.1 Stock List and Search

  1. Navigate to Inventory in the sidebar.
  2. The stock list displays all products with current quantities, unit cost, and expiry status.
  3. Use the Search bar to filter by product name or category.
  4. Use the Category dropdown to filter by Medication, Vaccine, Consumable, or Equipment.

5.2 Adding or Adjusting Stock

To add new stock (receive a delivery):

  1. Click Receive Stock.
  2. Search for the product or click New Product to create a new item.
  3. Enter the Quantity Received, Unit Cost, Supplier, and Expiry Date.
  4. Click Save.

To manually adjust stock (correction):

  1. Find the product in the list and click its row.
  2. Click Adjust Stock.
  3. Enter the adjustment amount (positive to add, negative to reduce).
  4. Enter an Adjustment Reason (required for audit trail).
  5. Click Confirm Adjustment.

Warning: Manual adjustments are logged and visible to Clinic Admin. Always provide an accurate reason.

5.3 Transfer Between Branches

For clinics with multiple branches:

  1. Click Transfer Stock.
  2. Select the Source Branch (your current branch).
  3. Select the Destination Branch.
  4. Search for the product and enter the Quantity to Transfer.
  5. Click Submit Transfer.

The receiving branch will see the transfer pending in their Incoming Transfers queue. They must confirm receipt to update their stock count.

5.4 Expiry Alerts

Items approaching expiry appear highlighted in the inventory list:

  • Yellow: Expires within 90 days
  • Red: Expires within 30 days or already expired

The Dashboard also shows a summary count of expiring items. Click the alert to jump directly to the affected products.

5.5 Barcode Scan (Tablet Camera)

On a tablet, you can scan a product barcode instead of typing:

  1. On the Inventory screen, click the Scan icon (barcode symbol).
  2. Point the tablet camera at the product barcode.
  3. The system identifies the product and opens its detail card.
  4. Proceed to receive, adjust, or view as needed.

Note: Barcode scanning requires camera permission to be granted in your browser settings.

6. Billing & Payment

6.1 Generating an Invoice

  1. Navigate to Billing in the sidebar, or click Create Invoice from a completed appointment.
  2. If starting from Billing, click New Invoice and search for the patient/owner.
  3. Line items from the appointment (consultation fee, medications dispensed) are pre-populated from the EMR prescription.
  4. Add any additional services or products by clicking Add Item.
  5. Review the subtotal and click Proceed to Payment.

6.2 Applying Loyalty Points and Discounts

On the payment screen:

  • Loyalty Points: The owner's available points balance is displayed. Toggle Use Points to apply them. Points convert to a baht discount at the configured rate.
  • Manual Discount: Click Add Discount, enter the amount or percentage, and select a reason from the dropdown.
  • Promotional Code: Enter a code in the Promo Code field and click Apply.

Note: Loyalty points are accrued per invoice. The owner earns points on the final paid amount after discounts. Redemption and earn rates are configured by the Clinic Admin in Settings.

6.3 PromptPay QR Code Generation

  1. On the payment screen, select QR / PromptPay as the payment method.
  2. The system generates a PromptPay QR code for the invoice total.
  3. Show or print the QR code for the client to scan with their banking app.
  4. Once the client completes payment, click Confirm Payment Received.

Tip: The QR code encodes the exact invoice amount — the client cannot enter a different value in their banking app.

6.4 Recording Payment

Select the payment method:

  • Cash: Enter the amount tendered. The system calculates change automatically.
  • Credit / Debit Card: Select Card and record the last four digits for the receipt. Note: automated card processing arrives in Phase 10 — currently record-only.
  • QR / PromptPay: Follow the flow in 6.3 above.
  • Split Payment: Click Split to divide the total across multiple methods.

Click Complete Payment to finalise. The invoice status changes to Paid.

6.5 Issuing a Receipt or Refund

Receipt: After payment is complete, click Print Receipt or Send Receipt via Email.

Refund:

  1. Find the original invoice in the Billing history.
  2. Click the invoice row and select Issue Refund.
  3. Enter the refund amount and reason.
  4. Select the refund method (cash or card).
  5. Click Confirm Refund.

Warning: Refunds reverse the loyalty points earned on that invoice. Partial refunds reduce points proportionally. This cannot be undone.

7. Clinic Settings & Admin

Note: This section is accessible to Clinic Admin role only.

7.1 Clinic Profile

  1. Click Settings in the sidebar.
  2. Select the Clinic Profile tab.
  3. Update the Clinic Name, Logo, Address, Phone, and Tax ID.
  4. Click Save Profile.

The clinic name and logo appear on all printed receipts and the login page.

7.2 Staff Management

Inviting a new staff member:

  1. Go to Settings → Users.
  2. Click Invite Staff.
  3. Enter the staff member's Email and select their Role (Receptionist, Doctor, Clinic Admin, or a custom role).
  4. Click Send Invitation.

The staff member receives an email with a link to set their password.

Assigning or changing roles:

  1. Find the staff member in the Users list.
  2. Click Edit on their row.
  3. Update the Role dropdown.
  4. Click Save.

Note: A staff member can hold multiple roles simultaneously. Permissions are the union of all assigned roles.

7.3 Notification Settings

  1. Go to Settings → Notifications.
  2. Edit the SMS Templates for appointment reminders, appointment confirmations, and prescription-ready alerts.
  3. Use placeholders such as {owner_name}, {pet_name}, {appointment_time} in templates.
  4. Click Save Templates.
⚠Coming Soon (Phase 11): SMS and LINE OA dispatch are not yet active. Templates can be configured but messages will not send until a real provider is connected.

7.4 Payment Methods Configuration

  1. Go to Settings → Payments.
  2. Toggle on the payment methods you accept (Cash, Card, PromptPay).
  3. For PromptPay, enter your PromptPay ID (mobile number or Tax ID).
  4. Set the Loyalty Points earn rate (baht per point) and redemption rate.
  5. Click Save Payment Settings.

7.5 Branch Management

  1. Go to Settings → Branches.
  2. The list shows all branches under your clinic tenant.
  3. Click Add Branch to create a new location.
  4. Fill in the branch Name, Address, and Phone.
  5. Assign a Branch Manager from existing staff.
  6. Click Save Branch.

Note: Each branch has its own inventory, staff assignment, and appointment calendar. Revenue reports can be viewed per branch or aggregated.

8. Common Quick Workflows

Workflow 1 — New Patient First Visit

This end-to-end flow covers everything from the moment a new client walks in to checkout.

  1. Register the owner: Go to Pets → New Owner. Enter name, phone, email. Click Save Owner.
  2. Add the pet: On the owner profile, click Add Pet. Enter pet details and chip ID if available. Upload a photo. Click Save Pet.
  3. Book the appointment: Go to Appointments → New Appointment. Select the pet, choose the doctor, pick the time slot, and set the type to First Visit / Consultation. Click Book Appointment.
  4. Check in the patient: When the client arrives, click the appointment on the calendar and click Check In.
  5. Open the EMR: Click Open Chart from the appointment. The doctor records SOAP notes and uses the anatomy canvas if needed.
  6. Prescribe medications: Under the Plan section, click Add Prescription. Search and add medications. Click Finalize Prescription — inventory deducts automatically.
  7. Generate invoice: Click Create Invoice (the appointment fee and dispensed medications are pre-filled).
  8. Apply discounts/loyalty: The new patient has no points yet. Apply any first-visit promotional discount if configured.
  9. Collect payment: Select payment method, complete payment, and click Print Receipt.

Workflow 2 — Follow-Up Visit

For a returning patient, most information is already on file.

  1. Search for the patient: In Pets, type the owner's name or the pet's name in the search bar. Click the pet's row to open the profile.
  2. Open the appointment: Navigate to Appointments and locate today's booking for this patient. Click Check In.
  3. Update SOAP notes: Open the chart. The doctor adds new notes to the Objective, Assessment, and Plan fields. Previous visit history is visible in the timeline below.
  4. Dispense medications: Add or update the prescription. Click Finalize Prescription to deduct stock.
  5. Generate and pay invoice: Create the invoice, apply the owner's available loyalty points if they choose, select the payment method, and complete payment.
  6. Print or email receipt: Click Print Receipt or Send via Email.

Workflow 3 — Low Stock Response

When an inventory alert appears on the Dashboard:

  1. Acknowledge the alert: Click the Inventory Alerts widget on the Dashboard. The system shows items at or below the reorder threshold.
  2. Review stock levels: Click any flagged item to see current quantity, reorder point, and last received date.
  3. Create a purchase order (external): Contact your supplier. Record expected delivery date in the item notes.
  4. Receive the stock: When the delivery arrives, go to Inventory → Receive Stock. Search for the product, enter the received quantity and expiry date. Click Save.
  5. Verify updated count: Confirm the stock level has updated correctly. The Dashboard alert clears automatically once the quantity exceeds the reorder threshold.

Tip: Set reorder thresholds for each product in its detail view (Inventory → [product] → Edit → Reorder Point). The system then generates alerts proactively before you run out.

9. Troubleshooting & FAQ

Forgot Password

There is currently no self-service "forgot password" link on the login page. If you cannot remember your password:

  1. Contact your Clinic Admin and ask them to reset your password from Settings → Users.
  2. Log in with the temporary password they give you.
  3. Once logged in, go to Preferences → Change Password and set a password only you know.

Note: Resetting your password revokes all of your existing login sessions on every device, as a security measure.

"Session Expired" Error

Your session has a maximum duration of 8 hours. When it expires, the system shows a Session Expired message.

Resolution: Click Back to Login and sign in again. Any unsaved data in open forms may be lost — click Save Draft periodically when writing long SOAP notes.

Cannot Find Patient in Search

If searching by name returns no results:

  1. Check spelling — try the first three letters only to broaden the search.
  2. Search by phone number or chip ID instead.
  3. The patient may be archived — ask a Clinic Admin to restore the record if needed.
  4. If the patient is new, they may not be registered yet — use New Owner to create the record.

PromptPay QR Not Generating

If the QR code screen shows an error or blank image:

  1. Confirm that the PromptPay ID is configured in Settings → Payments.
  2. Check that the invoice total is greater than zero.
  3. Refresh the payment screen and try again.
  4. If the problem persists, collect payment by cash or card and inform your Clinic Admin to verify the PromptPay configuration.

Inventory Count Mismatch

If the system's stock count does not match physical stock:

  1. Go to Inventory and find the affected product.
  2. Click View Adjustment Log to see all recent changes (prescriptions, manual adjustments, transfers).
  3. Identify the discrepancy source.
  4. Use Adjust Stock to correct the count, providing a reason in the required field.
  5. If the mismatch appears to be a system error, note the product name and the expected vs actual count and contact support.

Tip: Conduct a physical stock count at the start of each month and reconcile against the system. This prevents large discrepancies from accumulating.

Support Contact

For issues not covered in this manual:

  • In-app help: Click the Help icon (question mark) in the top navigation bar.
  • Email support: Contact your clinic's designated Anemal support representative.
  • Clinic Admin escalation: Your Clinic Admin has access to the system audit log and can investigate most access or data issues without contacting external support.

Note: Platform-level issues (login system down, billing/subscription problems) must be escalated by your Clinic Admin to the Anemal platform team. Clinic staff cannot access platform administration.

Admin Setup Guide

First-time onboarding for Clinic Owner / Clinic Admin. Complete all sections in order — estimated total time: ~75 minutes.

1. First-Time Login (~5 min)

What to Expect

When you log in for the first time you will land on the Dashboard. Because no data has been entered yet, every panel will show zero counts or empty states. This is normal. The system is waiting for you to complete the setup steps described in this guide.

Steps

  1. Open your clinic's Anemal URL (provided in your welcome email).
  2. Enter the Username and Password from that email, then press Sign In.
  3. On first login you will be prompted to change your password. Choose a strong password (minimum 8 characters, at least one number).
  4. You will land on the Dashboard with an empty state view.

Language Toggle

The interface is available in English and Thai. To switch:

  1. Click your profile avatar in the top-right corner of the top navigation bar.
  2. Select Language from the dropdown.
  3. Choose English or ภาษาไทย. The change applies immediately without a page reload.

4. Appointment Configuration (~10 min)

Troubleshooting

Port already in use

Symptom: Error: listen EADDRINUSE :::4000 or :::5173

# Linux/macOS
lsof -ti :4000 | xargs kill -9

# Windows PowerShell
netstat -ano | findstr :4000
taskkill /PID <pid> /F

Database connection refused

Symptom: PrismaClientInitializationError: Can't reach database server

  1. Confirm the container is running: docker ps --filter name=vetclinic-pg
  2. If stopped: docker start vetclinic-pg
  3. Confirm DATABASE_URL in src/backend/.env matches the container's host/port/credentials exactly (host and port are machine-specific — see the callout above).

JWT errors (invalid signature / jwt malformed)

  1. Ensure JWT_SECRET is set and not the placeholder string.
  2. If you changed JWT_SECRET after seeding, log in again — old tokens are now invalid.
  3. Confirm the client sends Authorization: Bearer <token>.

Prisma migration failures

  1. Check the database is reachable (see above).
  2. Dirty migration state: npx prisma migrate resolve --rolled-back "<migration-name>", then re-run npm run db:migrate.
  3. Full dev reset (drops all data): npx prisma migrate reset — runs migrations + seed.
  4. After any schema change: npm run db:generate.

Backend refuses to start — missing CRON_SECRET / SETTINGS_ENCRYPTION_KEY

Both are required at boot. Generate valid values with openssl rand -hex 32 and set them in src/backend/.env.

Node version mismatch

  1. Check with node -v — the project targets Node 20 LTS.
  2. nvm install 20 && nvm use 20
  3. Reinstall dependencies in both packages after switching.

Layered Backend Architecture

Routes → Controllers → Services → Repositories → Prisma Client (PostgreSQL)
  • Controllers are thin — parse the request, call a service, shape the response. No business logic.
  • Services hold all business logic. No direct Prisma calls.
  • Repositories are the only layer that touches Prisma. Every function takes tenantId as an explicit parameter — never inferred implicitly.
  • Full pattern whitelist, error taxonomy, and transaction-boundary rules: .claude/standards/architecture-rules.md.

Multi-Tenancy (absolute rule)

🚨Every query on a tenant-scoped table includes WHERE tenant_id = :tenantId, taken from the authenticated JWT — never from the request body or a query parameter. Cross-tenant access returns 404, never a 403 that would confirm the record exists elsewhere. No FK in the schema itself enforces this (a deliberate, ADR-recorded trade-off — see docs/adr/0028), so every relation traversal (an include, a raw-SQL join) must carry its own tenant predicate; a standing conformance test enforces this across the codebase. @db-agent reviews every database-touching change and holds veto power.

Authorization — Two Planes

PlaneRoutesToken carriesNotes
Clinic/clinic/*, /clinic-admin/*, /settings/*tenantId, branchId, effective roleIds[]/permissions[] (resolved via /auth/me)Built-in roles (Clinic Admin, Doctor, Clinic Staff) or a custom role cloned from one, capped at the assigner's own permission set
Platform/platform/*platformUserId, no tenantIdThe SaaS operator's console — customer provisioning, plans/quotas, cross-tenant audit. Never touches clinic PII except one narrow, audited exception (ADR-0015).

Every protected route calls requirePlane(...) then either requirePermission('module.action') (clinic) or requirePlatformPermission('platform.*') (platform) — deny-by-default, server-enforced. Frontend guards are UX convenience only. Full permission catalogue and the default role matrix: anemal-rbac-matrix skill.
